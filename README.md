🏆 world-championship-2023-risk-evaluation
This project is a data-driven risk analysis of the World Championship 2023, focusing on evaluating performance, injury risks, financial exposures, and competitive uncertainties associated with major international sports tournaments. 
Using real-world data and predictive modeling, this repository aims to provide insights into strategic risk management during high-stakes competitions.

📌 Overview
The World Championship 2023 Risk Evaluation project dives deep into the dynamics of elite competition by applying data science techniques to assess and visualize various forms of risk.
These risks span across player performance, team dynamics, injury likelihood, scheduling conflicts, and even external factors like travel and geopolitical tension. The ultimate goal is 
to inform decision-makers, analysts, and fans about possible outcomes and uncertainties that could impact the integrity and success of the tournament.

📂 Dataset
This analysis uses data compiled from:

Official match statistics and performance metrics

Historical injury records

Environmental and travel data

Betting odds and expert predictions

News sentiment and social media signals

Scheduling and location-based variables

Note: All datasets used are either publicly available or anonymized to preserve privacy and proprietary rights.

🧠 Features of the Project
📊 Risk Evaluation Dashboard: Interactive charts showing performance and injury probabilities by team/player

🔍 Predictive Modeling: Machine learning models to predict outcomes and simulate risk under various scenarios

🧾 Scenario Analysis: Hypothetical situations and how they affect tournament progression

🌍 Geo-Risk Mapping: Analyzing risks based on venue, travel paths, and environmental factors

💥 Injury Risk Models: Logistic regression and time-series forecasting for injury prediction

🔐 Confidential Risk Index (CRI): Custom index representing risk levels by team, match, and round
